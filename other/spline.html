ABOUT

Eclectica 2K26 is the annual technical symposium organized by the Department of Electronics & Communication Engineering. We bring together students, professionals, and innovators to celebrate technology, creativity, and innovation.

Join us on March 13, 2026, for a day filled with technical competitions, workshops, and networking opportunities that will help shape the future of technology.
