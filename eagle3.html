About Us

ECLECTICA 2k26 is the flagship annual department fest that brings together brilliant minds, creative talents, and innovative thinkers. Our fest is a celebration of diversity, technical prowess, and cultural excellence.

With a legacy of spectacular events spanning technical competitions, cultural performances, workshops, and networking opportunities, ECLECTICA has become the most anticipated fest of the year. Join us for three days of innovation, inspiration, and unforgettable experiences.

Whether you're a tech enthusiast, an artist, or simply someone who loves to explore new horizons, ECLECTICA 2k26 has something special for everyone.
